Physics-Informed Deep Learning for Rapid Efficiency Map Prediction and Cycle-Oriented Optimization of IPMSM

Rapidly predicting efficiency maps and minimizing energy consumption by learning physical laws.

📌 연구 개요 (Overview)

We propose a physics-informed deep learning framework that separates iron and copper losses to rapidly predict efficiency maps and minimize energy consumption over driving cycles. It offers higher physical consistency and faster prediction speeds than purely data-driven models.

🧑‍💻 연구 내용 (Research Details)

  • Incorporating physical knowledge through separate modeling of loss components (iron loss, copper loss)
  • Implementation of rapid prediction algorithms for the entire operating region (efficiency map)
  • Integration with driving cycle simulation and optimization of energy consumption

🛠️ 주요 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Physics-Informed AI: Embedding physical laws into the loss function.
  • Efficiency Map: Rapid generation of motor efficiency contours.
  • Cycle Optimization: Minimizing total energy over specific driving cycles.
