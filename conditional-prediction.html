Multi-modal CNN IPMSM Design
Sine wave

Conditional Performance Prediction of IPMSM under Absence of Detailed Geometric Parameters: A Fusion of Cross-Sectional Image and Operating Conditions

Multi-modal deep learning predicting performance using only images and operating conditions.

📌 연구 개요 (Overview)

A multi-modal deep learning framework that precisely predicts IPMSM performance using only cross-sectional topology images (CNN) and macroscopic operating conditions (DNN) in the absence of detailed design parameters. This approach reduces prediction error by approx. 16.2% compared to traditional numerical models, maximizing efficiency in the conceptual design phase.

🧑‍💻 연구 내용 (Research Details)

  • Designing CNN architecture for processing motor cross-sectional topology
  • Fusion of DNN models for operating conditions (current, speed, etc.) and image features
  • Achieving performance prediction accuracy using only shape information without detailed dimensions

🛠️ 주요 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Multi-modal Learning: Combining Image (Visual) and Scalar (Numeric) data.
  • CNN & DNN: Feature extraction and regression.
  • Image Processing: Preprocessing motor topology images.
