📝 personal research

2-Stage Deep Learning Surrogate Framework Combining Classification and Regression for Optimal Design of Electric Machines

DNN/CLASSIFICATION/MOTOR ANALYSIS

Developed a 2-Stage Deep Learning Surrogate Framework combining classification for geometric feasibility and regression for performance prediction to enable rapid and robust optimal design of IPMSM

View Details →
Conditional Performance Prediction of IPMSM under Absence of Detailed Geometric Parameters: A Fusion of Cross-Sectional Image and Operating Conditions

Multi-modal Deep Learning / IPMSM Design / Image Processing

A multi-modal deep learning framework that precisely predicts IPMSM performance using only cross-sectional topology images (CNN) and macroscopic operating conditions (DNN) in the absence of detailed design parameters. This approach reduces prediction error by approx. 16.2% compared to traditional numerical models, maximizing efficiency in the conceptual design phase.

View Details →
Physics-Informed Deep Learning for Rapid Efficiency Map Prediction and Cycle-Oriented Optimization of IPMSM

EM/EFFMAP/AI

We propose a physics-informed deep learning framework that separates iron and copper losses to rapidly predict efficiency maps and minimize energy consumption over driving cycles.
