Jeong Hyeon Yeom

I am an M.S. student at Chung-Ang University, specializing in electric machine design ⚡️.

I received my B.S. degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from Konkuk University.

My research focuses on e-motor design, multi-physics simulation, and data-driven workflows for design exploration. I am particularly interested in building fast and reliable surrogate models to accelerate large-scale DOE and optimization.

Currently, I am developing AI-assisted performance prediction for high-speed traction motors. Ultimately, I aim to contribute to technology development that benefits future generations.
