Smart Prosthetic Hand with Driving Assistance Functions

Built a prosthetic hand in collaboration with mechanical engineering teammates.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Collaborated with mechanical engineering teammates to build a smart prosthetic hand equipped with special functions to assist forearm amputees in driving.

🧑‍💻 역할 (Role)

  • Embedded system circuit design for prosthetic hand operation
  • EMG sensor signal processing and motor control algorithm implementation
  • Development of driving assistance mode logic and integration testing
  • App development and electromagnetic simulation support

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • EMG Control: Muscle signal-based finger movement.
  • Driving Mode: Specialized grip for steering wheel control.
  • Embedded System: Real-time processing with MCU.

🏆 결과 및 성과 (Outcomes)
