Smart Prosthetic Hand with Driving Assistance Functions

Built a prosthetic hand in collaboration with mechanical engineering teammates.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Collaborated with mechanical engineering students to build a smart prosthetic hand for forearm amputees. Equipped with driving assistance functions to enhance real-life utility.

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Arduino Control: Motor control and signal processing.
  • EMG Sensors: Muscle signal detection for hand movements.
  • Mechanism Design: 3D printed parts for grip and driving assistance.

🏆 결과 및 성과 (Outcomes)

  • Participated and demonstrated in the 24th Creative Design Competition
  • Enhanced hardware design and control skills through collaboration with mechanical engineering students
