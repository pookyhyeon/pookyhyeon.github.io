Policy Smart Grid Safety

Policy Diversification for EV Charger Safety in Smart Grid Environments

Industry analysis and policy proposal project for future charging safety.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Derived policy proposals to ensure the safety of EV charging infrastructure in a smart grid environment. Analyzed future industrial environments and proposed effective safety regulations.

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Industry Analysis: Analysis of future smart grid and EV charging trends.
  • Policy Proposal: Development of safety regulations and diversification strategies.
  • Safety Standards: Review of existing electrical safety standards.

🏆 결과 및 성과 (Outcomes)

  • Grand Prize (Minister of MOTIE Award)
  • Enhanced understanding of electrical safety policies and proposal skills
