Policy Diversification for EV Charger Safety in Smart Grid Environments

Industry analysis and policy proposal project for future charging safety.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Analyzed safety issues of EV chargers in smart grid environments and proposed institutional mechanisms and policies to prevent them.

🧑‍💻 역할 (Role)

  • Team leader managing and coordinating the entire project
  • Identifying potential hazards in smart grid environments and proposing safety policies
  • Analysis of electrical safety regulations and policies

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Policy Analysis: Review of current safety standards.
  • Risk Assessment: Identifying hazards in V2G/Smart Grid.
  • Proposal: New safety protocols for future infrastructure.
