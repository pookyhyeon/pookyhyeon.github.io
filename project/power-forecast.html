Electricity Demand Forecasting for Revitalizing Rural Areas

LSTM-based power consumption prediction for rural regions.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Analyzed power data in rural areas and predicted power consumption using LSTM algorithms to explore efficient energy management strategies.

🧑‍💻 역할 (Role)

  • Power consumption data preprocessing and pattern analysis
  • Design and training of LSTM prediction model based on MATLAB
  • Verification of prediction accuracy and model optimization

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Data Preprocessing: Cleaning and normalizing time-series data.
  • LSTM Model: Deep learning for sequential data prediction.
  • MATLAB: Implementation using Deep Learning Toolbox.

📸 프로젝트 진행 과정 (Project Process)

🏆 결과 및 성과 (Outcomes)
