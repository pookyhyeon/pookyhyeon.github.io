Vehicle Sensor Development for the Hearing-Impaired

Drowsy-driving detection and sound visualization using deep learning & microprocessor.

📌 프로젝트 개요 (Overview)

Developed a system that visualizes sound for hearing-impaired drivers to recognize road conditions and prevents drowsy driving.

🧑‍💻 역할 (Role)

  • Training and optimization of deep learning models for drowsy driving detection
  • Development of real-time data processing system based on microprocessor
  • Sensor hardware interfacing and visualization module integration
  • Circuit design and prototype fabrication

🛠️ 주요 기능 및 기술 (Technical Features)

  • Sound Visualization: Visualizing external sounds for driver awareness.
  • Drowsiness Detection: Deep learning-based driver monitoring.
  • Microprocessor: Real-time processing and system control.

📸 프로젝트 진행 과정 (Project Process)

🏆 결과 및 성과 (Outcomes)

  • Excellence Award (President of Konkuk University Award)
  • Encouragement Award (Hanyang Univ. Engineering Education Innovation Center)
  • 2023 KU SW Competition Jury Special Award
  • Proposed engineering solutions for the socially vulnerable
